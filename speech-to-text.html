ℹ️ How to Use:

1. Click "Start Recording" and allow microphone access
2. The app will automatically detect if you're speaking Portuguese, English, or Spanish
3. Speak clearly into your microphone
4. Watch the "Detected Language" indicator show your language
5. Click "Stop Recording" when finished
6. Use "Copy Text" to copy the transcription to clipboard
7. Use "Clear" to reset and start over
